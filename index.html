A few taps per frame

  1. Tap near the match ball in the large picture.
  2. Tap its exact centre in the magnified view. Use + and − to fit the outline.
  3. Choose Visible or Partly covered, then Save & next.

For a covered ball, outline only the visible part. Mark “Cut off” when the ball reaches the edge of the image.

Use Before and After to understand the movement. Your label always belongs to This frame. If you can't see a ball, choose Not visible and a reason. Use Unsure for a blob you can't identify.

On a computer, use 1–4 for visibility, Enter to save and arrows to nudge the marked centre. Hold Shift to nudge five pixels.
