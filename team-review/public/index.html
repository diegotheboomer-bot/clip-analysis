Zoom, outline, save

  1. Pinch with two fingers to zoom into the match ball. Move both fingers together to pan.
  2. Drag one finger from one corner of the ball to the opposite corner to draw a box. Drag again to replace it.
  3. Choose Visible or Partly covered, then Save & next.

For a covered ball, outline only the visible part. Mark “Cut off” when the ball reaches the edge of the image.

Use Before and After to understand the movement. Your label always belongs to This frame. If you can't see a ball, choose Not visible and a reason. Use Unsure for a blob you can't identify.

On a computer, use Ctrl + mouse wheel to zoom. Use 1–4 for visibility, Enter to save and arrows to nudge the marked centre. Hold Shift to nudge five pixels.
